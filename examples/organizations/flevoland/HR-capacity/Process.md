# Management Capacity Claim Process

## Prepare
When the manager has a capacity issue, he or she enters into a discussion with the HR Business Partner and possibly the Personnel Controller (it is important that they are involved from the outset).

- A meeting follows between the Manager, the HR Business Partner (who monitors, among other things, the objective and annual program), and the Personnel Controller (who monitors, among other things, the financial assessment framework).
- After the aforementioned meeting, the manager (or unit or team employee) writes the capacity request for the Management Board in the Core Proposal for Management Capacity Claim.

## Request
The manager sends the capacity claim (no later than Friday 12:00 PM) to the Secretary of the Board of Directors. The Secretary ensures that it is placed on the next Board of Directors meeting agenda.

## Decide
The Board of Directors makes a capacity decision on a weekly basis.

- In the event of rejection, the request is returned to the manager.
- Upon agreement, the following applies:
    - the hiring decision goes to procurement (Legal Affairs and Procurement unit)
    - the staffing decision goes to recruitment (HRM unit).

    In both cases, we follow the process as agreed upon there, with the understanding that HRM always plays a role.

    In the case of hiring the planning and control officer of the relevant unit/team is engaged.

## Administer
The financial controllers enter the Board of Directors' decision into the financial software package (as a reservation); this applies to both staffing and hiring.
When the employee has started employment via staffing, the reservation can be removed after one month, as the reservation for salary costs is then booked as actual expenses.
For hiring, the reservation can be converted into an obligation upon the actual incurring of that obligation.
